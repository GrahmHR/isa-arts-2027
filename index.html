Carla Mazefsky, Ph.D.
Licensed Clinical Psychologist, Nancy J Minshew MD Chair in Autism Research and Professor of Psychiatry at the University of Pittsburgh
Presentation
Emotion Dysregulation and Suicide in Autism: Understanding Risk and Improving Assessment
+Read more

Autistic people experience high rates of emotion dysregulation and suicide risk, yet distress may not always be visible or captured well by conventional assessment. This talk will explore what we know about emotion dysregulation and suicide in autism, how autistic experiences may shape vulnerability, and why more autism-informed approaches to assessment are needed. Emerging research and practical considerations for recognizing and assessing risk will be discussed.

Dr. Mazefsky directs the Regulation of Emotion in Autistic Adults, Children, and Teens (REAACT) Research Program at the University of Pittsburgh. Her research focuses on understanding and assessing emotion regulation and mental health in autism across the lifespan, with particular emphasis on emotion dysregulation, measure development and suicide risk. She directs the Pittsburgh Autism Center of Excellence, which is focused on adult mental health and suicide in autism.
